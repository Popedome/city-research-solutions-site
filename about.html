Our Story

Over Five Decades of Insight-Gathering Excellence

City Research Solutions is a full-service custom marketing research and consulting firm established in the Madison, Wisconsin area in 1979. Every engagement is run by a full bench of senior insights professionals, each with decades of experience and no junior hand-offs, serving clients from Fortune 100 companies to emerging technology ventures.

Our team specializes in measuring consumer attitudes and behaviors, providing clients with meaningful insights across various touchpoints — including retail environments, residential settings, online platforms, and our own world-class research facilities.

Whether you need qualitative depth or quantitative rigor, we offer both, tailored to your specific research objectives and business goals.
