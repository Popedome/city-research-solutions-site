Retail Research Techniques

Insights at the Point of Purchase

Obtaining insights at the retail level can be extremely meaningful for consumer product companies. Our retail research methods provide direct observation and measurement of consumer behavior at the shelf, supporting category reviews, decision tree analysis, pricing strategies, and packaging development.

Our Retail Methods

Shop-a-longs

Accompany consumers on their shopping trips to observe real decision-making at the shelf

Packaging Effectiveness Testing

Evaluate packaging impact on purchase decisions and shelf visibility

Point of Purchase Interviews

Intercept interviews capturing decision factors in the moment of purchase

Simulated Retail Shopping

Controlled shopping environments to test pricing, placement, and merchandising strategies
