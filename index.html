Pervasive Insights™: the AI suite built on your research.

Every study we field for you keeps working after the readout. Pervasive Insights™ puts your research history in a private portal: a searchable library and data corpus, synthetic panelists calibrated to how your customers actually answer, and a monthly read on your competitive market. Human research first; machine learning built on it. A City Research Solutions product.

The difference in one line

Other synthetic tools invent consumers from a demographic filter. Ours are calibrated identity profiles built from the studies you have already fielded, with the calibration error reported topic area by topic area.
