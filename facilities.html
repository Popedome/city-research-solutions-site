World-Class Focus Facilities

Modern research facilities in downtown Janesville, Wisconsin, featuring stadium-style client suites for comfortable observation during research sessions.

Our Spaces

Explore Our Facilities

Each space is designed to facilitate productive research sessions while keeping clients comfortable and engaged.

01

Group Facilities

Our main focus group room measures 24 x 32 feet and can comfortably accommodate up to 20 respondents. The versatile space is adaptable for various research formats including traditional focus groups, triads, and large central-location testing sessions.

  • 24 x 32 ft adaptable space
  • Seats up to 20 respondents
  • Multiple configuration options
02

Client Viewing Suite

Four dedicated observation rooms seating up to 20 client-observers with complete privacy. Stadium-style seating provides excellent sightlines to the focus group room, ensuring every observer has a clear view of the research in progress.

  • 4 observation rooms
  • Seats up to 20 observers
  • Stadium-style seating
03

Client Work Lounge

A comfortable, professional space designed for private conversations, concurrent meetings, post-session debriefs, and focused work. Equipped with secure internet access so your team stays productive between sessions.

  • Private meeting space
  • Secure internet access
  • Debrief-ready environment
04

Test Kitchen Facilities

A fully functional kitchen equipped for food and beverage testing, product preparation, and consumer taste-testing sessions. Features one-way glass with an adjacent client viewing area for unobtrusive observation.

  • Fully functional kitchen
  • One-way glass observation
  • Adjacent client viewing area
05

Respondent Waiting Area

Separate, comfortable waiting facilities for research participants. Designed to keep respondents relaxed and at ease before their sessions, ensuring they arrive in the best possible frame of mind for productive research.

  • Comfortable seating
  • Separate from research areas
  • Welcoming atmosphere
