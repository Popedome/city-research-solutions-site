Pervasive Insights™ · Synthetic customers

Surveys, focus groups and one-on-ones with calibrated synthetic panelists.

Build a panel from your own segments, then field a survey, run a moderated group, or interview a single panelist in depth. Every result carries its calibration state, and the whole panel is graded on a report card you can open any time.

Pervasive Insights is a synthetic research panel built on a client's own human research: a searchable library of every study the client has run, and synthetic respondents calibrated to how that client's real customers answered, with the error reported topic by topic. It is a City Research Solutions product. City Research has run consumer research since 1979.

Mode

Panel Survey

Field a survey to 50 to 500 calibrated synthetic panelists, sized to each client. See results by total sample or by segment.

Mode

Focus Groups

Run moderated focus group discussions with synthetic panelists from any segment. Group count and participants per group are set for each client; delivered as a transcript and a report.

Mode

One-on-One

Interview a single panelist in depth about their category behavior. Chat with a panelist in their own voice.

Panel builder

Not invented consumers. Your consumers, calibrated.

Every panel is drawn from calibrated synthetic panelists built on your fielded research: the segments your surveys found, the language your focus groups used, the trade-offs your trackers measured. A Market Representative Panel mirrors your buyer population; a Custom Panel lets you set the segment mix, gender mix and age group, and add recruiting criteria in plain language.

Where you have tested, you get your exact human results. Where you have not, you get a calibrated read that respects everything you have tested, with realistic person-to-person variation rather than a flat average. When something is genuinely new, the panel flags it low-confidence and points to a real test.

Coffee maker replacement (sample) · Groups: 3 · Mode: round-robin

Group 1 (3 participants)

MODERATOR: Welcome everyone. I'm going to go around the room on each question so everyone gets a chance to share. Dana, your turn.

Dana T. [Pragmatic] The carafe. I was making a pot for one person and pouring half of it out. The new one does a cup and I stopped feeling guilty.

Marcus W. [Confident] Opposite for me. If it can't make ten cups when my in-laws show up, it's a toy.

Priya N. [Considered] I don't care about either until it goes on sale. Then I care whether it fits under the cabinet.

MODERATOR: Marcus, how much does capacity matter versus the price?

Marcus W. [Confident] If it holds ten cups I'll pay forty more. If it holds six I won't pay anything.

Report · Topline

Of 9 participants, 5 named single-cup flexibility as the replacement trigger, 3 named guest capacity, 1 named counter footprint. Price surfaced as a gate rather than a driver: nobody replaces until a sale, then fit and capacity decide.

Under the hood · Calibration Report Card

The question isn't "can you build a synthetic panel." It's "does it answer like my customers."

Every client's portal has a report card: how closely the synthetic panel's answer matches real customer data for each of 19 marketing topic areas, for each segment, measured in percentage points. It updates as new waves of real customer research come in.

94.7%

Rows fully passing: all 5 segment gaps ≤ 5pp

How to read this page

Each cell is the gap between the synthetic panel and real customer data for that segment, in percentage points. Green is within ±5pp; amber is wider and flagged; rows that are not yet calibrated say so. Calibration is ongoing, and the card updates as new waves of real customer research come in.

Across all clients, on calibration fit: about 3.5pp average error, 92% of 95 cells within 5pp. On blind holdout, which is the prediction number, the median miss is about 6.8 points with 42% of cells within 5. For scale: when a real survey is split randomly in half, the two halves agree within 5 points on about 85% of answers (58 consumer studies, 41,340 answer pairs). No synthetic method can beat a human study against itself, and Pervasive Insights reports both numbers on the same report card. Explore the Constellation view →

Under the hood · Constellation

Every marketing topic area, and where its calibration stands.

The Constellation is the map behind the report card. Each point is one of the 19 marketing topic areas the portal calibrates. The closer a point sits to the center, the more of your own human data anchors it; the color says how far calibration has gone.

  • Measured · calibrated directly against your fielded studies
  • Inferred · read from a related topic area; flagged in every result
  • Not yet calibrated · the portal says so, and points to the human study that would fix it

Every new study you field with us moves points inward. The live view opens from the portal sidebar under Under the hood.

What a week with it looks like

Cheap kills, fast iteration, field the finalists

The concept funnel

Forty concepts, budget to field eight. The panel screens all forty on Monday, kills the bottom of the pile with the evidence attached, and the eight fielding slots go to survivors instead of favorites. Real respondents crown the winner.

The design round

Twelve ideas for a seasonal line. Drop them all, get the segment splits and the obvious kills by lunch, iterate the survivors ten times if you like, then field the best three. The fielded winner goes back into the research stack for next season.

The off-wave question

The tracker fields in March; the question arrives in July. Ask the panel before the meeting ends. Where the report card shows a segment cell in amber, a five-question pulse fields exactly the flagged items instead of the whole wave.
