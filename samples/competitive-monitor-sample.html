A purchase diary follows a few dozen to a few hundred real buyers in the category. Each one logs a purchase when it happens, photographs the receipt, and answers a short follow-up: what else they considered, what came second, and why it lost. AI-moderated follow-up interviews make this far cheaper and faster than it used to be.

Social listening is software that reads what people post publicly about a category, including reviews, social posts, videos and forums, and counts what is being said and how it changes. It is the earliest signal and the least precise. Use it to spot movement, then confirm it with the sweep and explain it with the diary.
