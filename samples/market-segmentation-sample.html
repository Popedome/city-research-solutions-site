How the segments were built

Sample. Online survey of 1,512 US adults who bought home fitness equipment or a paid home workout app in the past 24 months, weighted to the category buyer profile on age, sex, region and income. Inputs. 34 needs and attitude statements plus workout frequency and equipment owned. Method. Latent class analysis; solutions from three to seven segments were compared on fit, stability across random halves and whether a marketer could act on the differences. The five-segment solution was the most stable and the easiest to describe. Spend. Self-reported, with receipts checked on a subsample of 240. Total-sample margin of error is about ±2.5 points; within a segment it is ±4.5 to ±6.5 points.

Each profile answers the same six questions: who they are, what they buy, where they buy it, what motivates them, what stops them, and where to reach them. Figures are the share of that segment unless noted. Choose a segment above or click a card.

A typing tool is a short set of questions that assigns any new respondent to a segment, so future surveys, customer lists and sales conversations can use the same five groups without repeating the full 34-item battery. These eight questions were selected from the survey and tested on a holdout sample of 378 respondents the model never saw.

Summary

The group liked that the Fold is quiet and stores against a wall. Mark and Alicia want to know it is gentle on knees and backs before anything else. Denise was the most positive and drew the others toward the low noise. The price was accepted once the group heard there is no required subscription.

Emotional tone

Cautious interest, with practical worry about joints and getting on and off the seat.

Why it matters

A segmentation usually goes stale after the readout. A live panel keeps the segments working for every question that comes next: a concept, a price point, a claim, a name.

How it is built

Synthetic panelists are calibrated against your own segmentation survey, segment by segment, and weighted to each segment's share of US category buyers.

When to go back to people

Use the panel to narrow choices and sharpen questions. When a decision is big enough, take the short list back to real buyers.
