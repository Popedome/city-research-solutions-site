Tamber, a fictional small-appliance brand, sells a well-reviewed air fryer that loses more often than it should. To see why, 40 people about to buy an air fryer kept a diary on their phone for three weeks. Every time anything touched the decision, a video, a store aisle, a friend's kitchen, a review, they logged it in the moment: a photo or a short video, a line of text and two quick ratings. The diary kept going through purchase and the first uses at home.

Why in the moment

Memory tidies the storyAsked a month later, people name two or three steps. The diary logged a median of 31.
Small moments get lostA box too tall for the counter, a review read at midnight. They decide sales and rarely survive recall.
The photo is the evidenceA tape measure against the cabinet says more than "I worried about size."

The journey map is built from the entries themselves. Each entry was tagged to a stage and a touchpoint. Percentages are the share of participants who logged that touchpoint at least once in that stage. The first three stages use all 40; purchase and first use use the 36 who bought.

Seeing it beats reading about it

The two strongest positive moments, a friend's kitchen and an open display, both let people see the basket and the food. Neither is something a product page offers today.

Size worries arrive late

Counter and cabinet space came up in comparing, after a favorite was chosen. 11 people photographed a tape measure. Most could not find the height of the model they wanted.

One bad review travels

The coating complaint was logged by 13 people, usually after sorting reviews by lowest stars. It followed the leading brand and rarely touched Tamber.

Tamber is easy to find at the shelf and hard to find anywhere earlier. Shortlists are set while people compare, and by then most had never seen a Tamber. When it did make the list, it won more often than any other brand.

What it means for Tamber

Get on the list
in the first week

The product converts. The problem is being missing while lists form. Put Tamber in the recipe videos people watch in the first days, publish the inside basket size and the height with the drawer open, and push for open displays where people can pull the basket out. Test a photo of the basket after six months of use as an answer to the coating worry.
