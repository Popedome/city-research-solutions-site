How this test was run

Online survey, September 8 to 19, 2026. 1,500 US adults 18+ who bought a frozen single-serve meal in the past 30 days, split into five matched cells of about 300 (balanced on age, gender, region and household size). Monadic: each person saw one board only, so no idea is judged against another. Significance is a two-tailed test of proportions at 95% confidence. The norm band is the middle range of earlier frozen meal concept tests run to the same questionnaire.

These are the boards respondents saw, word for word. Each board has a name, a one-line idea, three benefits and a price, with no photography, so the test measures the idea and not the styling. The current best seller was written up in exactly the same format to serve as the benchmark. Each person saw one board.

Boards are written to the same length and reading level. Prices are suggested retail per meal.

Purchase intent is the headline measure: after reading the board, how likely are you to buy it? The top two box is the share who said "definitely" or "probably" would buy. Letters show which cells a score is significantly higher than. With about 300 per cell, a gap needs to be roughly eight points to count.

A concept test picks the idea. It does not prove the product. These are the steps between this readout and a launch decision, in the order we would run them.

Recommended next steps

1
Fix the heat message on Night Market Noodles

Add a mild, medium, hot marker to each of the six styles and retest the board with a quick claims check among buyers 45 and over. Target: cut "too spicy" worry from 17% to under 10%.

2 weeks
2
Confirm the price

Three matched cells at $4.29, $4.79 and $5.29. Value for money sits inside the norm at $4.79, but 11% named price as a dislike.

3 weeks
3
In-home use test of two recipes

About 150 category buyers cook and rate the product at home. The board promises firm noodles and bright sauce. The product has to deliver that, or repeat purchase will not follow trial.

5 weeks
4
Rework Garden Forward Bowls for wave two

It leads with buyers 55+ and scores highest on relevance. Find a sharper point of difference and retest alongside a refreshed current bowl.

Next wave
5
Retire Comfort Minis and park Skillet Finish

Minis would mostly cannibalize. Skillet only returns if the pan step can be removed.

Now

Decision needed

Approve development of Night Market Noodles into recipe work and the first three next steps, with a go or no-go on launch after the in-home test.
